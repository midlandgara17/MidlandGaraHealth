BEFORE YOU MESSAGE

Help us understand what you need.

When contacting us about a health concern, you can briefly explain your main concern and, where relevant, provide your age group, how long the problem has been present and any important symptoms.

Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information. Do not use WhatsApp messaging as a substitute for emergency medical care.

URGENT MEDICAL PROBLEMS

Some situations need immediate care.

If someone has severe difficulty breathing, severe chest pain, sudden weakness or numbness, loss of consciousness, seizures, severe bleeding or another potentially life-threatening emergency, seek urgent medical care at the nearest appropriate emergency facility.
