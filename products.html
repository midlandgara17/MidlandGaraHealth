USE RESPONSIBLY

A device is a tool — not a diagnosis.

Home monitoring can help you understand your health information, but unusual or concerning readings should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

Important:

Smart watches and other consumer devices may provide estimates rather than clinically validated measurements. For important medical decisions, use appropriate validated equipment and professional assessment.
